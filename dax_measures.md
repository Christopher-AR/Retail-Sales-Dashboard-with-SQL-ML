# Key DAX Measures 

This document highlights the core DAX measures used to derive business insights and drive decision-making in the dashboard.

## Total Sales
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Superstore[Customer_ID])

Counts the number of unique customers.

## Total Orders
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Superstore[Order_ID])

Represents the total number of orders placed.

## Repeat Customers
Repeat Customers = 
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT(Superstore[Customer_Name]),
    FILTER(
        VALUES(Superstore[Customer_Name]),
        CALCULATE(COUNT(Superstore[Order_ID])) > 1
    )
)

Identifies customers who made more than one purchase.

## Orders per Customer
Orders per Customer = DIVIDE([Total Orders],[Total Customers])

Measures average purchase frequency per customer.

## Profit Margin
Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit],[Total Sales])

Shows profitability as a percentage of revenue.

## YoY Growth %
YoY Growth % = 
VAR SelectedYear = SELECTEDVALUE(Superstore[LYSYear])
RETURN
IF(
    ISBLANK(SelectedYear),
    BLANK(),
    DIVIDE(
        [Total Sales] -
        CALCULATE(
            [Total Sales],
            FILTER(
                ALL(Superstore),
                Superstore[LYSYear] = SelectedYear - 1
            )
        ),
        CALCULATE(
            [Total Sales],
            FILTER(
                ALL(Superstore),
                Superstore[LYSYear] = SelectedYear - 1
            )
        )
    )
)

Measures year-over-year growth in revenue.

## Customer Rank
Customer Rank = 
RANKX(
    ALL(Superstore[Customer_Name]),
    [Total Sales],
    ,
    DESC,
    DENSE
)

Ranks customers based on their revenue contribution.

## Top 20% Contribution %
Top 20% Contribution % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Top 20% Sales],
    [Total Sales]
)

Shows how much revenue is generated by the top 20% of customers.

## Sales Insight
Sales Insight = 
VAR Growth = [YoY Growth %]
RETURN
IF(
    Growth > 0,
    "📈 Sales increased by " & FORMAT(Growth,"0%") & " compared to last year",
    "📉 Sales declined by " & FORMAT(ABS(Growth),"0%") & " compared to last year"
)

Provides a quick insight into overall sales trend.

## Customer Insight
Customer Insight = 
VAR Contribution = [Top 20% Contribution %]
RETURN
IF(
    Contribution > 0.6,
    "⚠️ High dependency: Top 20% contribute " & FORMAT(Contribution,"0%"),
    "✅ Balanced distribution: Top 20% contribute " & FORMAT(Contribution,"0%")
)

Highlights customer loyalty and retention health.

## Most Profitable Category
Most Profitable Category = 
CALCULATE(
    SELECTEDVALUE(Superstore[Category]),
    TOPN(
        1,
        VALUES(Superstore[Category]),
        [Total Profit],
        DESC
    )
)

Identifies the category generating the highest profit.

## Least Profitable Category
Least Profitable Category = 
CALCULATE(
    SELECTEDVALUE(Superstore[Category]),
    TOPN(
        1,
        VALUES(Superstore[Category]),
        [Total Profit],
        ASC
    )
)

Highlights the category contributing the least profit.
